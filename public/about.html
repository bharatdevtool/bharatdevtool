About Bharat Dev Tools

Free, fast tools for developers, students and everyday work. Built in India for everyone.

What we build

Bharat Dev Tools is a set of more than 50 free online tools that open instantly and need no sign-up. Every tool runs inside your browser, so what you paste or upload never leaves your device. It is used by developers, students, job and exam applicants, small businesses and anyone who needs to fix a PDF, photo or file quickly.

Developer tools

  • JSON: formatter and validator with grid view and JSONPath queries, JSON Diff, JSON to TypeScript, YAML ↔ JSON, CSV ↔ JSON.
  • Encode and decode: Base64, URL encoder, JWT decoder, hash generator, UUID generator.
  • Text and code: regex tester, generator and library, diff checker, HTML formatter, Markdown to HTML, lorem ipsum.
  • API and apps: cURL tester and comparison, deep link launcher, cron expression generator, timestamp and timezone converters.
  • Design: color picker, CSS gradient generator, favicon generator, Lottie player, converter, validator and optimizer.

PDF tools

  • Edit and sign: PDF editor, sign PDF, redact PDF, watermark, page numbers, PDF metadata.
  • Organise: compress, merge, split and organise pages, grayscale PDF.
  • Convert and protect: PDF to JPG or image, JPG or image to PDF, password-protect and unlock, share a PDF that expires.

Image, QR and everyday tools

  • Resize a photo or signature to an exact size in KB, with presets for UPSC, SSC, NEET, JEE, IBPS and other exam and government forms.
  • Image compressor, resizer and PNG ↔ JPG converter.
  • QR code generator and decoder, WhatsApp link generator.
  • GST, EMI, BMI and percentage calculators.

See every tool on the homepage.

How we work

  • Private by design: processing happens on your device. We have no servers that receive your input. See the privacy policy for exactly what anonymous statistics we collect.
  • Free and no sign-up: no account, no paywall, no ads.
  • Fast: pages load quickly and work on phones and slow connections.
  • Works everywhere: any modern browser on Android, iPhone, Windows, macOS or Linux. Nothing to install.
  • Accurate: strict parsers and clear error messages.
