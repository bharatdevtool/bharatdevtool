Everything you need to know about the BMI Calculator

BMI (Body Mass Index) is a numerical value derived from a person's height and weight. The formula is:

Metric: BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height² (m²)

Imperial: BMI = 703 × weight (lb) ÷ height² (in²)

It provides a simple, inexpensive screening measure to categorise individuals as underweight, normal weight, overweight, or obese.

The standard WHO BMI categories are:

  • Below 18.5 — Underweight
  • 18.5 – 24.9 — Normal weight
  • 25.0 – 29.9 — Overweight
  • 30.0 – 34.9 — Obese Class I
  • 35.0 – 39.9 — Obese Class II
  • 40.0+ — Obese Class III
