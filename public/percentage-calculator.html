Everything you need to know about the Percentage Calculator

A percentage calculator is a free online tool that helps you quickly compute percentages without manual formula work. It covers common use cases: finding what percent of a number is, calculating percentage increase or decrease, finding percentage difference between two values, and working out what percentage one number is of another.

To find what percentage X is of Y, divide X by Y and multiply by 100. Formula: (X ÷ Y) × 100 = result%. For example, 25 is what percent of 200? (25 ÷ 200) × 100 = 12.5%. Use the "X is what % of Y?" tab above for instant results.

Percentage change = ((New Value − Old Value) ÷ Old Value) × 100. A positive result means an increase; a negative result means a decrease.

Example: Price went from ₹500 to ₹600. Change = ((600 − 500) ÷ 500) × 100 = 20% increase.

Yes, completely free. No registration, no account, no payment required. All calculations happen instantly in your browser with no data sent to any server.

Percentage change measures how much a value increased or decreased relative to the original value — it has a clear "before" and "after".

Percentage difference measures the relative difference between two values where neither is the baseline — it uses the average of both values as the reference: |A − B| ÷ ((A + B) ÷ 2) × 100.
