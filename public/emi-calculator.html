Everything you need to know about EMI calculation

EMI (Equated Monthly Instalment) is the fixed monthly payment you make to repay a loan over a specified period. It is calculated using the formula:

EMI = [P × R × (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N – 1]

Where: P = Principal loan amount, R = Monthly interest rate (Annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100), N = Number of monthly instalments (Tenure in months).

Example: For a ₹10,00,000 loan at 9% annual interest for 20 years — R = 9/12/100 = 0.0075, N = 240. EMI ≈ ₹8,997/month.

An amortization schedule is a complete month-by-month table showing how each EMI payment is split between principal and interest over the entire loan tenure. In the early months, most of your EMI goes toward interest. As you repay the loan, the interest component decreases and the principal component increases. This calculator generates the full amortization table for your loan.

There are three main ways to reduce your monthly EMI:

  • Increase the loan tenure — A longer repayment period means smaller EMIs, but you pay more total interest.
  • Negotiate a lower interest rate — Even a 0.5% reduction can significantly lower your EMI. Compare rates from multiple lenders.
  • Make a larger down payment — Reducing the principal loan amount directly reduces your EMI.
