Everything you need to know about the Word & Character Counter

A word counter is an online tool that counts the number of words, characters, sentences, and paragraphs in a piece of text. It works by splitting your text on spaces and punctuation to identify word boundaries, then counting each unit. Our free word counter also calculates reading time based on an average reading speed of 200 words per minute.

Yes, this word counter is completely free. There is no sign-up, no registration, and no hidden fees. You can count words and characters for unlimited amounts of text at no cost.

Your text is completely safe. All word and character counting happens entirely in your browser — your text never leaves your device and is never sent to any server. This makes it safe to use with sensitive or confidential content such as draft articles, legal documents, or private notes.

Reading time is estimated based on the average adult reading speed of approximately 200 words per minute (WPM). The word count is divided by 200 to give the estimated time. For very short texts, the time is shown in seconds instead of minutes.

Yes — the character counter works for all languages including Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Bengali, and other Indian scripts since it counts Unicode characters directly. The word counter works best for space-separated text. For Devanagari or other Indic scripts, the character count is the most accurate metric to use.
