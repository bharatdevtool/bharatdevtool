How to use

  1. Paste your HTML into the input box (or click Load sample).
  2. Choose Beautify to format with indentation, or Minify to strip whitespace.
  3. Pick your preferred indent style (2 spaces, 4 spaces, or tab).
  4. Click the button — the result appears instantly.
  5. Copy to clipboard or download as an .html file.
