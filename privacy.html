Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2025

Welcome to Bharat Dev Tools

Your privacy is extremely important to us. This policy explains how we handle your information when you use our JSON Formatter, Beautifier, Escape/Unescape and Minify tools.

1. No Data Collection

  • We do not collect, store, or share any data you paste or upload into our tools.
  • All formatting and processing happen directly in your browser using JavaScript. Your data never leaves your device.

2. No Account Required

You don’t need to sign up or provide personal information to use the site. We do not store names, emails, or any identifiable data.

3. Analytics (Optional)

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand traffic patterns such as page visits, user interactions, and general usage statistics. These analytics do not collect content you paste into the tools.

  • IP addresses are anonymized for privacy protection.
  • Analytics tracking is automatically disabled on localhost/development environments.
  • No personally identifiable information is collected or stored.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings if you prefer not to be tracked at all. You can also opt out by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

4. Cookies

  • We use minimal cookies, for example to remember your editor font size preference.
  • If analytics is enabled, a cookie may be used to measure traffic anonymously.
  • We do not use cookies for advertising or profiling.

5. Contact Information

If you have any privacy‑related questions or requests, contact us at: feedback@bharatdevtools.com.

6. Policy Updates

We may update this policy to reflect changes in practices or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted here with a new “Last updated” date.

Summary

  • We don’t store your data
  • We don’t share your data
  • We don’t track personal information
  • All processing is done locally in your browser
