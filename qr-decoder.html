Everything you need to know about QR Code Decoder

A QR Code Decoder extracts information from QR code images. Simply upload a QR code image (PNG, JPG, or GIF), and the tool will analyze it to extract the encoded link, text, or data. The decoded information is displayed instantly, and you can copy it or launch it directly.

Upload a QR code image by clicking the upload area or dragging and dropping the image file. The tool will automatically decode the QR code and display the extracted link or text. You can then copy the link or launch it directly using the Launch button.

The QR Code Decoder supports common image formats including:

  • PNG (Portable Network Graphics)
  • JPG/JPEG (Joint Photographic Experts Group)
  • GIF (Graphics Interchange Format)
  • WebP (Web Picture format)

Maximum file size is 10MB for optimal performance.

The Launch button opens the decoded link in a new browser tab. If the link is a deep link or app link, it will attempt to open the associated app on your device. This is especially useful for testing deep links and app links.

Decoded QR codes are stored locally in your browser's storage for convenience (so you can access your history), but they never leave your device. All QR code decoding happens entirely in your browser, ensuring complete privacy and security.
