Everything you need to know about the Age Calculator

An Age Calculator is a free online tool that computes your exact age from your date of birth to today (or any custom date). It shows your age broken down into years, months, days, and also as total days, hours, and minutes. You can also use it to calculate the age or time difference between any two dates.

Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field and click Calculate Age. The tool instantly shows your exact age in years, months, and days, along with totals in days, weeks, hours, and minutes. You can also change the "Calculate age as of" date to find your age at any point in time.

Yes, the Bharat Dev Tools Age Calculator is completely free. No sign-up, no registration, no hidden fees. You can use it as many times as you like from any device — desktop, tablet, or mobile.

Your data is completely safe. All calculations happen entirely in your browser using JavaScript. No date-of-birth or personal information is ever sent to any server. The tool even works offline once the page has loaded.
