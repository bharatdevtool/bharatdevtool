Everything you need to know about the Reading Time Estimator

A reading time estimator calculates how long it takes to read a piece of text based on average reading speed. Paste or type your content and the tool instantly shows the estimated reading time in minutes and seconds. It is widely used by bloggers, content writers, and editors to add "X min read" labels to articles.

The average adult reads at about 200–250 words per minute (WPM) for general content. Slow readers average around 150 WPM, while fast or proficient readers can exceed 400 WPM. Academic or technical reading tends to be slower (100–200 WPM). This tool defaults to 238 WPM — the figure used by Medium and commonly cited in reading research. You can adjust the slider to match your personal reading speed.

Yes, completely free. No sign-up, no paywall, and no usage limits. Paste any length of text and get instant results. The tool will always remain free on Bharat Dev Tools.

Your text never leaves your browser. All calculations — word count, character count, reading time, and speaking time — run entirely in JavaScript on your device. No data is transmitted to any server, making this tool safe even for private, confidential, or sensitive content.

Speaking time is the estimated time to read text aloud — for example in a presentation, podcast, or speech. The average speaking pace is about 130 words per minute, which is slower than the typical silent reading speed of 238 WPM. Use speaking time to plan presentations, record voiceovers, or prepare for public speaking.
