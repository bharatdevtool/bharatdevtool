Everything you need to know about GST calculation in India

GST (Goods and Services Tax) is a unified indirect tax levied on the supply of goods and services in India, introduced on 1 July 2017. It replaced multiple cascading taxes like VAT, service tax, excise duty, and more. GST is calculated as a percentage of the taxable value. For intra-state transactions, GST is split equally into CGST (Central GST) and SGST (State GST). For inter-state transactions, IGST (Integrated GST) is charged at the full GST rate.

India has four main GST tax slabs:

  • 5%: Essential goods — packaged food, transport, footwear below ₹1,000, medicines
  • 12%: Processed food, computers, business class air travel
  • 18%: Most services, electronics, restaurants with AC, mobile phones above ₹12,000
  • 28%: Luxury goods, automobiles, tobacco, aerated drinks, casinos

Some essential items like fresh vegetables, milk, curd, and eggs are exempt (0% GST).

To add GST to a price (exclusive of GST):

  • GST Amount = Original Price × (GST Rate ÷ 100)
  • Total Price = Original Price + GST Amount

Example: Original price = ₹1,000, GST rate = 18%

GST Amount = ₹1,000 × 0.18 = ₹180

Total = ₹1,000 + ₹180 = ₹1,180

To find the original price when the quoted price already includes GST:

  • Original Price = Inclusive Price ÷ (1 + GST Rate ÷ 100)
  • GST Amount = Inclusive Price − Original Price

Example: Inclusive price = ₹1,180, GST rate = 18%

Original Price = ₹1,180 ÷ 1.18 = ₹1,000

GST Amount = ₹1,180 − ₹1,000 = ₹180

CGST (Central GST) and SGST (State GST) are charged on intra-state (within the same state) transactions. Each is exactly half of the total GST rate.

IGST (Integrated GST) is charged on inter-state (between different states) transactions and equals the full GST rate.

Example for 18% GST:

  • Intra-state: CGST = 9% + SGST = 9%
  • Inter-state: IGST = 18%
