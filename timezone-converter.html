Everything you need to know about the Timezone Converter

A timezone converter is a tool that lets you convert a date and time from one timezone to another. For example, you can convert 9:00 AM IST (India Standard Time) to the equivalent time in UTC, EST, PST, or any other timezone in the world. This is useful for scheduling meetings across different countries, coordinating with remote teams, or understanding when a global event happens in your local time.

India Standard Time (IST) is UTC+5:30, meaning it is 5 hours and 30 minutes ahead of Coordinated Universal Time (UTC). To convert IST to UTC, subtract 5 hours and 30 minutes. For example, 9:00 AM IST = 3:30 AM UTC. Our timezone converter handles this automatically — just select Asia/Kolkata as the source timezone and UTC as the target timezone, enter the time, and click Convert.
